Sample noise moves unregularized least-squares coefficients by up to ||epsilon||_2 / sigma_min, with sigma_min the smallest singular value of Phi, and a ridge term lambda > 0
Bounds the movement for an orthonormal basis, and leaves a nearly dependent basis as sensitive as before
Removes the effect of the noise, so the fits to noisy and to clean samples agree
Bounds the movement by a factor set by lambda for any basis, and shrinks the coefficient vector toward zero
Lowers the noise amplification and the bias together, so a larger lambda gives a better fit
